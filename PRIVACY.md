# Privacy policy: RYM Now Playing (Last.fm)

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

RYM Now Playing (Last.fm) does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no server of its own and no analytics.

## What is stored, and where
- **Your Last.fm username and refresh setting**: stored in Chrome sync storage (your Google account's extension storage), so they follow you across your own browsers.
- **Cached data**: your RYM username, the last track shown, and the RYM release matches you or the extension found. Stored locally in your browser only.

You can remove all of it by uninstalling the extension.

## Network requests
- **Last.fm** (`ws.audioscrobbler.com`): your Last.fm username is sent to fetch your recent scrobbles. Last.fm's own privacy policy applies.
- **RateYourMusic** (`rateyourmusic.com`): release searches and release pages are loaded from your logged-in session, and, only when you click **Set listening**, an update to your RYM play history is sent. RYM's own privacy policy applies.

No other requests are made. The extension never reads or stores passwords.
